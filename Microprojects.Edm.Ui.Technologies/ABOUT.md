# Technologies

Author the manufacturing process tree: profiles for hardware capabilities, devices and their hosts, multi-step processes with parameter extraction from profile JSON, and the technology references that the rest of the platform consumes.

A hierarchical workbench for the technologist — every recipe, routing, device contract and host wiring is owned, revised and audited here.

When Logistics receives a supply or an operator launches a job, the shape of what runs is defined in this module.
