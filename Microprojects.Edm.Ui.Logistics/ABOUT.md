# Logistics

Manage product flow from incoming supply to finished item: nomenclature definitions, tare lifecycle, supply receipts with batch genealogy, work orders and the operator desktop where shop-floor users execute jobs.

Master-detail browsers for technologists and admins; a kiosk-friendly desktop for operators — sharing one persistent inventory and the same audit trail end to end.

Repacking, allocation and grade tracking are first-class so material can move between tares, between cells, between stages without ever losing its history.
